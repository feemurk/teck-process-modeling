# Pillar 4: Logistics & Outbound Supply Chain

---

## Overview

Logistics & Outbound Supply Chain is the value realisation pillar — the processes by which physically produced concentrate is converted into a commercially invoiced and shipped product. The process boundary opens at filter cake reception in the concentrate shed and closes at issuance of the commercial invoice and Bill of Lading following vessel departure. The pillar encompasses four process areas: Concentrate Handling (site shed storage and blend management), Concentrate Transport (dispatch measurement and one or more transport legs to the export port by road, rail, slurry pipeline or sea), Port Operations (reception, stockpile management, and vessel loading), and Commercial Documentation (Certificate of Analysis, Bill of Lading, and commercial invoice).

This pillar represents the sharpest ISA-95 level transition in the value chain. Pillars 2 and 3 are dominated by ISA-95 Levels 0-2 (physical process, sensors, SCADA/DCS). Pillar 4 operates almost entirely at Levels 3 and 4 — discrete IT transactional workflows, commercial system integrations (ERP, trading platforms, port management systems), and documentary processes governed by contract law rather than process control. The few Level 0-2 touchpoints (weighbridge instrumentation, shiploader belt weigher, belt sampling equipment) are measurement points, not control loops.

Custody transfer is the governing concept of this pillar. Every process area is structured around which point legal and financial ownership of the concentrate passes from Teck to the buyer, and what measurement data constitutes the definitive commercial record at that point. AMIRA P754 Principles 5 and 9 (mass measurement at custody transfer; check-in/check-out mass balance) are the primary governance framework. The five custody transfer points span all four pillars — Pillar 4 contains the three that are commercially external (site-to-transport, transport-to-port, port-to-vessel) and these generate the highest-stakes transactional entities in the programme.

Transport routes and shipping cadence are configuration, not separate processes. A route is one or more transport legs, each with a mode (road, rail, slurry pipeline or sea); vessels are loaded directly at a berth or by lightering barges to a vessel at anchor; shipping is continuous year-round or seasonal where ice or weather closes the port. Seasonal shipping (e.g., Red Dog, where concentrate is stockpiled for months during the winter ice closure and shipped in a summer window of roughly July to October) creates long-duration open inventory positions, moisture management challenges and an annual or seasonal commercial cadence. Site examples in this pillar are indicative and are confirmed in each product's overlay.

## Process Decomposition

> **Column guide**: ISA-95 Level 3 = operations management (ERP, logistics systems, quality management). Level 4 = business planning and logistics (commercial, trading, shipping). Levels 0-1 appear only at weighbridge and shiploader measurement points. DDVC Layer: activities primarily generate high-value transactional entities in the Curated layer (custody transfer records, commercial documents) with Contextualized products consuming them for revenue and reconciliation analytics.

### 4.2 Concentrate Transport

> **Process patterns.** A site's route from mine to export port is one or more **transport legs**, each an instance of **4.2.2 Transport Leg** with its own mode — road (trucks), rail (wagons), slurry pipeline, or sea (barge or coastal vessel) — preceded by **4.2.1 Dispatch Measurement & Documentation** at its origin. The mode changes the instruments (weighbridge, pipeline flow and density, draft survey, GPS, AIS), not the steps. Routes, modes, carriers and measurement methods are configuration (`transport_leg`) — see *Commodity Variations*.

## Process Flow Narratives (BPMN-Ready)

### 4.1 Concentrate Handling — Process Flow Narrative

**Pool**: Concentrate Handling | **Lanes**: Filter Plant (Pillar 3 interface), Shed Operator, Logistics Coordinator, Metallurgist

**Start Event**: Filter cake being discharged from filter plant; shed feed conveyor active.

1. **Receive Filter Cake at Shed** (Shed Operator + DCS belt weigher) — wet tonnes received logged continuously; ERP inventory receipt posted by zone; **P754 CTP: Concentrator-to-Logistics internal handoff**.
2. **Assign Zone and Build Stockpile** (Shed Operator per logistics instruction) — FEL or conveyor directs incoming cake to assigned zone; ERP zone inventory incremented.
3. **Monitor Moisture in Shed Zones** (Metallurgist) — periodic grab samples submitted to LIMS at weekly interval per zone.
   - *[GATEWAY — Moisture approaching contract maximum?]* -> Yes: metallurgist initiates corrective action (turning, aerating; or expedite dispatch); alert to logistics coordinator. No: continue.
   - *[RED DOG LOOP — Arctic storage, weekly moisture check mandatory throughout winter storage period.]*
4. **Receive Dispatch Instruction** (Logistics Coordinator) — dispatch order issued from transport schedule; target lot size, commodity, destination, and required date specified.
5. **Optimise Blend for Dispatch** (Logistics Coordinator + Metallurgist) — zone grades and moisture reviewed; zone reclaim proportions determined to achieve target blend.
6. **Execute FEL Reclaim** (FEL Operator per reclaim plan) — concentrate reclaimed from designated zones in planned proportions; ERP zone inventory decremented per zone.
7. **Load to Transport Vehicle** (FEL Operator + Shed Operator) — concentrate loaded to truck or rail wagon; loading complete signal.

**End Event**: Transport vehicle loaded with concentrate; dispatch lot record created in ERP; vehicle ready for weighbridge.

### 4.2 Concentrate Transport — Process Flow Narrative

**Pool**: Concentrate Transport | **Lanes**: Weighbridge / Dispatch Operator, Logistics Coordinator, Transport Carrier / Pipeline Operator, ERP System

**Start Event**: Dispatch lot ready at the origin of a transport leg; dispatch measurement system operational and certified.

**[NOTE — The steps are the same for every mode. A route is one or more legs, each with a configured mode (road, rail, slurry pipeline or sea), carrier and measurement method (`transport_leg`).]**

1. *[GATEWAY — Discrete transport units?]* → Yes (truck, wagon, barge): **Record Transport Unit Empty Weight** (4.2.1.1). No (slurry pipeline): continue.
2. **Load Concentrate to Transport Unit** (4.2.1.2) — load by loader or conveyor, or re-pulp and pump; belt weigher or flowmeter cross-check.
3. **Measure Dispatched Quantity** (4.2.1.3) — weighbridge gross − tare, pipeline flow and density over the batch, or draft survey. **P754 CTP: Site-to-Transport** (first leg).
4. **Generate Bill of Lading** (4.2.1.4) — for the dispatch lot.
   - *[LOOP — repeat steps 1-4 for each unit or batch until the lot is complete.]*
5. **Dispatch Transport Unit and Issue Transport Documents** (4.2.2.1).
6. **Track In-Transit Shipment** (4.2.2.2) — GPS, rail tracking, pipeline SCADA or AIS.
   - *[GATEWAY — ETA deviation?]* → Yes: notify port and vessel scheduling; adjust if the vessel window is at risk. No: continue.
7. *[GATEWAY — Further leg on the route?]* → Yes: **Transfer Between Transport Modes** (4.2.2.3), then repeat from step 1 for the next leg. No: arrival at port → Port Reception (4.3.1).
8. **Monitor for Transit Loss** (4.2.2.4) — on each receipt measurement, per leg.

**End Event**: Dispatch lot arrived at port; ERP delivery status At Port; transit loss calculated for each leg.

### 4.3 Port Operations — Process Flow Narrative

**Pool**: Port Operations | **Lanes**: Port Logistics Manager, Port Weighbridge Operator, Port Operator, Shiploader Operator, Independent Surveyor, Sampling Technician

**Start Event**: Transport vehicle arrives at port; vessel expected per shipping schedule; berth allocated.

**[Reception Stream — runs as vehicles arrive]**

1. **Weigh Vehicle at Port Weighbridge** (Port Weighbridge Operator) — gross and tare weighed; port net wet tonnes calculated; transit loss vs. BoL computed. **P754 CTP: Transport-to-Port**.
2. **Log Port Receipt and Assign Shed Zone** (Port Logistics Manager) — ERP goods receipt posted; port shed zone assigned; inventory updated.
3. **Manage Port Stockpile** (Port Operator) — concentrate maintained by zone until vessel loading commences.
   - *[RED DOG: multi-week storage possible; moisture monitoring frequency increased.]*

**[Vessel Loading Stream — commences on vessel arrival]**

4. **Assign Berth and Inspect Holds** (Port Logistics Manager + Independent Surveyor) — NOR received; hold inspection completed and documented; any protested condition recorded.
5. **Conduct Initial Draft Survey** (Independent Marine Surveyor) — six-point draft measurement; initial displacement calculated; survey report signed by master.
6. **Commence Shiploader Operations** (Shiploader Operator + DCS) — concentrate reclaimed from port shed to shiploader; belt weigher logging; loading into hatches per stowage plan.
7. **Collect Continuous Belt Sample** (Sampling Technician) — auto-sampler collecting increments throughout loading; increments accumulating in primary sample cutter.
8. **Complete Loading and Seal Hatches** (Port Operator + Vessel Master) — all hatches loaded and sealed; mate's receipt per hatch; Statement of Facts drafted.
   - *[GATEWAY — Loading interrupted?]* -> Yes: document delay in Statement of Facts; assess laytime implications; restart when resolved. No: continue.
9. **Conduct Final Draft Survey** (Independent Marine Surveyor) — six-point draft measurement; final displacement calculated; cargo mass = final - initial - constants; commercial wet tonnes confirmed; survey report signed by master. **P754 CTP: Port-to-Vessel — FINAL CTP**.
10. **Split and Seal Belt Sample** (Sampling Technician + Independent Surveyor) — accumulated belt sample split three ways; seller's, buyer's, and umpire's portions sealed and labeled; chain-of-custody documented.
11. **Dispatch Umpire Sample to Independent Lab** (Logistics Manager) — umpire sample couriered to pre-agreed umpire lab; chain-of-custody receipt.

**End Event**: Vessel loaded and departed; final draft survey commercial wet tonnes confirmed; umpire sample dispatched; vessel loading record committed.

### 4.4 Commercial Documentation — Process Flow Narrative

**Pool**: Commercial Documentation | **Lanes**: Commercial Manager, Metallurgist, Independent Umpire Lab, ERP System

**Start Event**: Vessel loaded and departed; umpire sample dispatched; final draft survey commercial wet tonnes confirmed.

1. **Receive and Review Assay Results** (Metallurgist) — Teck's lab, buyer's lab, and umpire lab results received; compared per element against contractual split limits.
   - *[GATEWAY — All results within split limit?]* -> Yes: average of Teck and buyer used as CoA value. Some elements outside limit: umpire result used for those elements. All outside: umpire result used for all.
2. **Issue Agreed Certificate of Analysis** (Commercial Manager) — CoA finalised with split-determined values; signed by Teck's authorised representative; transmitted to buyer for acknowledgement; filed in ERP and LIMS.
3. **Confirm Commercial Quantity with Carrier** (Logistics Coordinator) — final draft survey commercial wet tonnes provided to carrier; carrier confirms for final BoL.
4. **Issue and Distribute Final Bill of Lading** (Carrier / Commercial Manager) — final BoL signed by vessel master; three originals distributed (Teck, buyer, buyer's bank if LC); BoL date is contractual shipment date for Incoterms.
5. **Calculate Payable Metal Content** (Commercial Manager) — smelter contract payability applied to CoA grades; TC/RC and penalty calculations performed; dry tonnes calculated from commercial wet tonnes and CoA moisture %.
6. **Apply QP Metal Price and Issue Provisional Invoice** (Commercial Manager + Finance) — LME/COMEX QP average price applied; provisional invoice issued at approximately 90% of estimated value; posted to ERP (FI — provisional revenue recognition).
   - *[INTERMEDIATE EVENT — QP period closes (typically 30-90 days after shipment).]* -> LME/COMEX final QP average price confirmed.
7. **Issue Final Invoice** (Commercial Manager + Finance) — final invoice calculated at confirmed QP price; adjustment to provisional invoice posted in ERP; final revenue recognition; payment expected per contract payment terms.
   - *[GATEWAY — Buyer disputes CoA or commercial quantity?]* -> Yes: formal dispute resolution per smelter contract terms (second umpire, re-assay, arbitration); invoice held pending resolution. No: invoice accepted and paid.

**End Event**: Final Invoice issued and payment received; commercial invoice posted in ERP (FI); vessel loading record commercially closed; shipment archived.
